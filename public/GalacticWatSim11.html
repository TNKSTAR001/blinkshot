⚔ Galactic War Sandbox

Command fleets from 6 factions with 25 unique ship classes: 10 fighters, 5 cruisers, 5 battleships, 3 dreadnoughts, the Leviathan Titan, and a faction Base of Operations.

Faction shipyards: every faction builds its fleet in a unique visual design language — Terran angular naval hulls, Frontier boxy industrial rigs, Zhen'Kari organic bioforms, Vex crystal shards, Corsair jagged raider blades, and Concord armored machine slabs. Every ship variant also carries its own hull glyph — turret rings, torpedo tubes, strike chevrons — so you can tell types apart at a glance.

1. Pick a faction — each has unique buffs & weaknesses.
2. Pick a ship class and hull type.
3. Click (or drag) on the map to deploy. Ships fight any other faction automatically.

Carrier Command: capital ships fabricate escorts with randomized variants and strictly limited lifetime stocks (no resupply once spent). Battleships summon 5 fighters total, Dreadnoughts 15 fighters total, and Titans 25 fighters + 5 cruisers total; each escort now arrives one at a time at 1-3s intervals. Carrier ships keep maneuvering while launching, except point-defence battleships (Aegis Bulwark), which are twice as fast, have a 50% smaller collision profile, and form a defensive line close to the centre of the battle-zone. Their 15% wider shields project from their current orientation, have 85% of dreadnought hull health, can be destroyed by any weapon, and cannot be re-deployed once broken.

Titan secrets: when battle bonuses are on and a faction fields a Titan, that faction secretly rolls one battle bonus for the current fight: Hive Swarm, Advanced Laser Cannon, Supernova Bomb, Advanced Missile Hangar, Guardian Matrix, or Nanite Bastion. Use Settings to enable or disable these effects.

Base of Operations: each faction can deploy a stationary command hub with double Titan hull and shield strength. Bases do not naturally repair hull health. Use Settings → Base Production to choose exactly which ships it can build, set per-ship active caps, adjust build rate/wave size/escort limit, and increase base health. Bases defend themselves with 3 mini chain cannons and 2 laser guns, and if a base is destroyed every other ship from that faction is destroyed with it.

Volatile wrecks: destroyed cruiser-class and larger ships have a 50% chance to erupt in a 2× explosion that damages nearby ships with radius and damage scaled to the destroyed hull size. Fighters only produce normal visual explosions.

Bomber runs: Hammer Bombers and Lancer Torpedo Boats launch two torpedoes one second apart, then make a wide circular circuit before the next drop. After six total torpedoes, they return to their producing ship or any friendly Titan, despawn into the bay for 4 seconds, and relaunch fresh with a full payload. Bombers actively hunt capital ships and refuse frontal assaults — they skirt outside a capital's gun range, whip around toward its flank or stern, and dive in to release their payload at maximum launch range.

Countermeasures: all ships carry one flare set. Fighters visibly dash away from slow shots when they can; if they are too slow or multiple missiles lock on, they spend that flare. Cruisers and up carry seeker countermeasures: cruisers 3, battleships 6, dreadnoughts 12, titans 24. Battleships and dreadnoughts keep anti-torpedo lasers, while Titans use missile-defense lasers that fire one shot every 0.5-1.5 seconds; those lasers only scratch ships.

Minefields: Thunderer and Breaker battleships seed up to 7 active electromagnetic mines across the battlezone when the Mines setting is on. They launch one mine every 10 seconds like a short-range missile, plant ahead of enemy paths, then arm and disable nearby enemy ships for 5 seconds. Only laser weapons can destroy mines before they trigger.

Fleet formation command: every faction now uses a unique fleet doctrine: defensive navies form walls, shells, or phalanxes, while aggressive factions build wedges, swarms, and raider spearheads. Every ship variant has its own formation role and adjusts its slot as more allies and mixed variants arrive.

Dogfighting: every fighter mounts a 360° secondary laser turret that zaps nearby enemies from any angle of attack. Fighters can rake multiple ships at extreme close range, while larger beam platforms project longer laser range and try to hold their firing envelope instead of drifting into point-blank combat.

Fleet spacing: friendly ships dynamically widen formation lanes by hull size, watch allied traffic ahead of them, and try to keep at least one ship length between allied hulls. When packed together, they slide sideways into open lanes instead of breaking formation; fighters and cruisers yield around heavier hulls rather than pushing them off course.

Capital broadsides: battleships, dreadnoughts, and titans close to a heavy-ship duel range, turn side-on, then slowly circle while firing. Titans seek titans first; other capital ships only prefer same-class broadsides when that same class is closer than a larger capital, otherwise they focus the largest enemy hull. Engaged ships avoid contact with side-slips instead of turning tail.

Scenario sizes: Random Skirmish and Domination can be launched as Small, Medium, Large, or Epic from the scenario size buttons. Domination creates two bases fighting over No Man's Land; the dominant team inside the zone captures it (Battlefield-style) — the capture bar fills toward whichever faction has more ships, and if the balance flips before it completes the progress rewinds at the same rate instead of resetting. Capture takes 30 seconds. A held zone must first be neutralized: a rival has to stay dominant for 5 seconds to break the hold before it can capture. Hold 3 minutes to win, or longest hold wins after 5 minutes. The zone border is grey and dotted while neutral and turns the owner's colour once captured. Domination fleets are scaled down to fit each side's half and start staged behind No Man's Land so neither team reaches it first, and Base Production is locked to fixed values (every 10s, 4 per wave, 16 active per base, x1 health, all classes) so neither side can out-produce the other.

Controls: Use Deploy, Move, Follow, Pilot, or Erase mode · Pilot uses WASD/Arrows to thrust in any direction while the mouse aims and faces the ship — so you can strafe sideways or reverse without turning (side-slip is 15% slower, reverse 30% slower) — and auto-fires the moment a foe sits under the crosshair (click a foe to lock) · right-click delete · mouse wheel zoom · middle-mouse or Shift+drag to pan · Space to pause · F or the Fleet Menu fullscreen button for full-page fullscreen · the eye button hides screen controls.
Touch controls: choose Deploy, Move, Pilot, or Erase · Pilot shows twin sticks after you tap one of your faction ships — the left stick thrusts in any direction — hold the right stick on a foe and the left stick lets you strafe sideways or reverse without turning (side-slip is 15% slower, reverse 30% slower) — the right stick aims and steers the crosshair, and guns fire automatically once a foe is in the crosshair (tap a foe to lock it) · two-finger drag to pan, pinch to zoom · open the ☰ Fleet Menu for all fleet options.
